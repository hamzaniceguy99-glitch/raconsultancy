What we do, in detail

Four services for businesses operating in Belgium, with reserved work referred out.

Program 01 · The order matters

Company set-up and administration

Setting up in Belgium has a sequence: the enterprise number, VAT activation, the registrations that apply to your activity, and the social obligations that start the moment you do. We keep the order and the deadlines, and we tell you which steps need a notary, an accountant or a social secretariat rather than us.

  • Per project
  • New and foreign-owned businesses
  • Quoted in writing
  • Identity documents

Program 03 · Sworn work referred

Translation and interpreting

Correspondence, commercial documents, websites and meetings. One thing to be clear about: a sworn or certified translation for official use needs a translator entered in the national register, and we are not that — we will tell you when your document needs one and refer you rather than supplying something an authority will reject.

Back-office support

We open the post, tell you what each item actually requires, and handle the parts that are administrative rather than regulated — flagging immediately anything that needs an accountant or a lawyer.
