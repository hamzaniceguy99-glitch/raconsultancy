How we work

A Brussels firm, clear about reserved professions

RA Consultancy is a company incorporated under Belgian law, registered with the Crossroads Bank for Enterprises under enterprise number 0802.743.294, based at 75 Drève du Château Box 3 in 1083 Ganshoren, Brussels.

Belgium reserves several activities to licensed professionals, and we respect every one of those lines. We do not keep books, prepare statutory accounts or advise on tax — that requires ITAA registration, which we do not hold. We give no legal advice, which belongs to the Bar. Payroll goes through an approved social secretariat. And a sworn translation for official use needs a translator entered in the national register, so we refer those rather than supplying something an authority will reject. Our filed activities also include pharmaceutical retail and sports instruction; we carry on no activity needing a separate authorisation unless it is actually held.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can you do our bookkeeping?

No. In Belgium, keeping books and advising on tax for third parties is reserved to professionals registered with the ITAA, and we are not registered. We will refer you to someone who is — and be wary of any consultancy that offers it without mentioning this.

Can you review our contract?

No. Legal advice and drafting are reserved to members of the Bar. We can explain what a letter is asking you to do administratively, and anything legal goes to an avocat.

I need a certified translation for a commune. Can you do it?

Not a sworn one. Official translations require a translator entered in the national register of sworn translators, and we will refer you. Supplying an ordinary translation for an official purpose simply gets it rejected.

Which language do you work in?

French, Dutch or English, agreed at the start. In Brussels that matters more than people expect, because the language of an official letter is not always the language you operate in.

Can you handle our payroll?

Not directly. Payroll in Belgium runs through an approved social secretariat, and we coordinate with one rather than doing it ourselves.

Your filed activities mention pharmacy and sports. Do you do those?

Those codes are on our registration, and we carry on no activity that requires a separate professional authorisation unless we actually hold it. For pharmacy in Belgium that means a pharmacist, which is a different thing entirely.
