Terms of service

What we provide

RA Consultancy provides business support, management consultancy and translation services provided by RA Consultancy, a company incorporated under Belgian law and registered with the Crossroads Bank for Enterprises under enterprise number 0802.743.294. IMPORTANT: the company does not keep accounts, prepare statutory financial statements or give tax advice for third parties. In Belgium those activities are reserved to professionals registered with the Institute for Tax Advisors and Accountants (ITAA), and the company is not so registered; such work is referred to a registered accountant or tax adviser. The company is not a law firm and provides no legal advice, legal drafting or representation, which are reserved to members of the Bar; legal questions are referred to an avocat. Payroll administration is carried out through an approved social secretariat rather than by the company. Translation is provided as ordinary commercial translation: a sworn or certified translation for official use requires a translator entered in the national register of sworn translators, interpreters and translator-interpreters, and where a client needs one the work is referred to a sworn translator. Interpreters remain impartial and interpret what is said without adding or advising. Although the company's registered activities include the retail sale of pharmaceutical products and sports instruction, it carries on no activity requiring a separate professional authorisation, licence or diploma unless that authorisation is actually held. Services are described on the Services page. No physical goods are sold or shipped through this site.
